THE AUTHOR & HIS STORY

Lloyd A. Vermont Sr.

FJIM · Self-taught investor · Mentor · Author

A self-taught, dividend-focused investor who believes financial knowledge should be shared across generations.

From modest beginnings to decades of mentoring others, Lloyd A. Vermont Sr. has built his investing philosophy around learning, ownership, patience and the potential of money put to work over time.

Explore his story ↓
01 / THE JOURNEY

Curiosity became conviction.

Lloyd A. Vermont Sr. describes growing up far from financial privilege. A voracious reader, he came to believe that success depends not on extraordinary gifts, but on learning and applying useful ideas.

He is quick to distinguish himself from the scholarly investment experts whose research he studies. Instead, he encourages ordinary people to benefit from that knowledge, develop their own understanding and make informed choices for their financial lives.

His approach is rooted in the conviction that more employees and consumers should have the opportunity to own a stake, however small, in the businesses and economies around them. For that reason, he sees practical financial literacy as an important part of education.

02 / THE PHILOSOPHY

Three ideas. One long-term view.

Lloyd A. Vermont Sr. invites readers to learn from investing principles, make them their own and consider what patient ownership can make possible.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Put money to work

Beyond earning wages, he encourages people to understand how owning shares in businesses can give money a role in building their financial future.

02 / TIME

Reinvest and be patient

Long-term ownership, dividend reinvestment and compounding form the heart of his approach. Returns vary, and time does not eliminate investment risk.

03 / LEGACY

Pass knowledge forward

He believes financial literacy and investing habits can be shared with younger generations, helping families build greater choice over time.

03 / GENERATIONS

Investing knowledge is a legacy to share.

40+Years of mentorship
Nearly 50Young investors mentored

Over more than 40 years, Lloyd A. Vermont Sr. says he has mentored nearly 50 young investors. He describes helping them open stock-market accounts to learn firsthand about ownership, dividend reinvestment and the role of time.

He has used this approach in his own financial journey, and his definition of wealth is intentionally broad. Wealth need not mean becoming extraordinarily rich. It can also mean having the freedom to move to a better financial position by choice.

Among the experiences he recounts is one investment account with a reported compound annual growth rate of 40.09% over 16 years.

Figures in this section are based on the author's account. The performance example is an individual historical outcome, not a typical result or guarantee of future returns.

04 / SERVICE & IDEAS

Beyond the stock market.

Alongside investing and mentoring, Lloyd A. Vermont Sr. has contributed to Jamaica's professional and civic life. A Fellow of the Jamaican Institute of Management (FJIM), he has served in leadership roles across professional associations and public institutions.

His supplied biography also describes voluntary service on the Market Research Committee of the Jamaica Stock Exchange, with interests that include historical market data and greater access to dividend reinvestment arrangements for investors and their families.

A longstanding commentator on Jamaica's development, he compiled decades of essays in Fifty-Five Years of Suggestions for a Better Jamaica, described in his biography as a 2020 collection of newspaper writings from 1964 to 2019.
